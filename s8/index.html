360° walk-through without WebGL

Drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom, and tap a white ring to walk into the next room. The camera turns to the door, moves forward through it, and the next room fades in around you as you arrive. Everything here is plain CSS 3D with a small script. No WebGL, no canvas 3D, no plugin.

Living room
Drag to look around · tap a ring to walk

Why no WebGL

Each room is a cube of six flat images placed with CSS 3D transforms. The browser's compositor draws them on the GPU, the same path it uses for scrolling, so it runs smoothly on older phones and laptops where WebGL viewers stutter or fail to start.

The move-in transition

The rooms are real 3D boxes, so the camera can travel inside them with correct perspective. A move turns the view to the door, glides forward, and cross-fades into the next room, which starts slightly behind its centre and settles in. Duration and easing are one setting each.

Your renders drop in

  • The rooms here are drawn in code as placeholders.
  • Your 360 renders (equirectangular or six cube faces) replace them; equirectangular images are cut into faces once, at build time.
  • Camera positions from your camera file set each room's place on the plan and where its doors point.
